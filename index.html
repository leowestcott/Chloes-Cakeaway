The art of cake making

It started with a birthday cake for Dad.

Chloé made her very first novelty cake for her dad’s 65th birthday. By her own admission it wasn’t a masterpiece — but it set her on a path she never expected to follow.

In 2016 the hobby became a business, and that same year she made her own wedding cake, because her husband had faith she could. Since then Chloé’s Cakeaway has baked for birthdays, anniversaries, christenings and every kind of milestone, learning something new with every cake.

Dad still gets a cake every year. Ten years on from that first attempt, it was a giant custard cream.

“Just learning as I go along in my little kitchen in Potters Bar.”

The Cakeaway way

Every cake is designed around one person and one celebration, then baked, filled, piped and finished by hand.

  • Designed around you

    Their name, their colours, their favourite things, the dog, the football team, the holiday. Your idea is the starting point.

  • Handmade details

    Figures and toppers are often modelled by hand and fully edible, and there’s a range of personalised card toppers too.

  • Dietary needs considered

    Gluten-free, dairy-free and vegan cakes have all come out of Chloé’s kitchen. Mention any requirements when you enquire.

  • Licensed & registered

    Fully licensed, registered and insured, with a food hygiene rating of 5 from Hertsmere.

How ordering works

From first idea to first slice.

  1. Tell Chloé the plan

    The occasion, the date, roughly how many people and any ideas or inspiration you have. Pinterest pictures welcome.

  2. Talk it through

    Chloé will get back to you to chat through the design, flavours, sizes and price.

  3. Made to order

    Once you’ve agreed the details together, your cake is baked and decorated for your date.

  4. The big reveal

    Collection or delivery is arranged with you directly, ready for the celebration.

Sending an enquiry doesn’t book a date — nothing is confirmed until you’ve heard back from Chloé and agreed the details together. The earlier you get in touch, the better.
